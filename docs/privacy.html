Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Kalorie app ("Kalorie", "we", "us") collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. Kalorie is a nutrition, workout, and weight tracking app.

Information we collect

CategoryWhat & why
Account info When you sign in with Apple, Google, or GitHub, we receive your name and email address from that provider to create and identify your account.
Health & fitness data Food logs, nutrition/macro data, body weight entries, and exercise/workout logs you create in the app. This data is stored on your device; if you have Kalorie Premium, it is also synced to our servers so it's available across your devices.
Photos Photos you take of food or nutrition labels using the in-app camera are sent to our servers to estimate nutrition information via AI. These photos are not stored — they are deleted immediately once the analysis is complete.
Purchase data Subscription and purchase status is handled by our payments partner, RevenueCat, on our behalf. We receive your subscription status but not your payment card details, which Apple and RevenueCat handle directly.
Advertising data Free-tier users may be shown ads via Google AdMob to unlock bonus scans. Google may process device and usage information to serve and measure these ads. We request non-personalized ads where supported.

Who we share data with

  • Apple, Google, GitHub — for sign-in authentication.
  • RevenueCat — to manage subscriptions and entitlements.
  • Google AdMob — to serve rewarded ads to free-tier users.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • Most of your data lives locally on your device unless you're a Premium subscriber using cloud sync.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us (see below).
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime from your Apple ID settings.

Children's privacy

Kalorie is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy, or want your data deleted? Email support@kalorie.fit.

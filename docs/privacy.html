Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Kalorie app ("Kalorie", "we", "us") collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. Kalorie is a nutrition, workout, and weight tracking app.

Information we collect

CategoryWhat & why
Account info When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address from that provider to create and identify your account. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
Health & fitness data Food logs, nutrition/macro data, body weight entries, and exercise/workout logs you create in the app. This data is stored on your device; if you have Kalorie Premium, it is also synced to our servers so it's available across your devices.
Photos Photos you take of food or nutrition labels using the in-app camera, plus any note you add, are sent to our servers and on to Google’s Gemini API to estimate nutrition information. We do not store these photos; they are discarded once the analysis is complete.
Food and exercise lookups When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product. When you browse exercises, your search terms are sent to ExerciseDB. Food search uses a database built into the app and sends nothing.
Bug reports If you send a bug report from Settings, your description, an optional screenshot you choose, your device platform and version, and your account ID are delivered to a private Discord channel that only the developer reads.
Purchase data Subscription and purchase status is handled by our payments partner, RevenueCat, on our behalf. We receive your subscription status but not your payment card details, which Apple and RevenueCat handle directly.
Advertising data Free-tier users may be shown ads via Google AdMob to unlock bonus scans. Google may process device and usage information to serve and measure these ads. We request non-personalized ads where supported.
Website waitlist If you enter your email on kalorie.fit to be notified at launch, we store it only to send that notice.

Who we share data with

  • Apple and Google (Firebase Authentication) — for sign-in.
  • Google (Gemini API) — to estimate nutrition from food and label photos.
  • Open Food Facts — to look up scanned barcodes.
  • ExerciseDB — for the exercise catalog.
  • Discord — to deliver bug reports you choose to send.
  • RevenueCat — to manage subscriptions and entitlements.
  • Google AdMob — to serve rewarded ads to free-tier users.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • Most of your data lives locally on your device unless you're a Premium subscriber using cloud sync.
  • Kalorie gives nutrition estimates for general wellness tracking. It is not medical advice.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account in the app.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime from your Apple ID settings.

Children's privacy

Kalorie is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

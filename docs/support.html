Support

Nutrition, workout, and weight tracking — here to help if something's not working right.

Contact us

Email support@kalorie.fit for account issues, billing/subscription questions, or feature requests. To report a bug, you can also use Settings → Support → Report a Bug in the app and attach a screenshot. We typically reply within a few days.

How do I sign in?

Kalorie uses Sign in with Apple or Google, so there's no separate password. The first time you sign in, your account is created automatically.

How do I log food?

Tap the + button. Search finds foods in the built-in USDA database, and the barcode button there scans packaged products. Analyze opens the camera: photograph a meal, or switch to Label to read a nutrition label. AI estimates the calories and macros, and you can edit them before adding to your log.

How do I track workouts and body weight?

Open the Progress tab. Use Add Weight Log to record your weight, and Search Workouts to find an exercise, log your sets (weight × reps) and see your strength over time. Tap the heart on an exercise to keep it on your Progress tab.

How do I cancel my Kalorie Premium subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID, not inside the app. On your iPhone: open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Kalorie, then choose Cancel Subscription. You can also manage this from the App Store app under your profile.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Kalorie, tap your avatar to open Settings, and under Subscription tap "Restore purchases." Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Why do I have a daily limit on AI food scans?

Each scan runs an AI model, which costs us money. Free accounts get 5 AI scans per day, and you can watch a short ad for 1 extra scan, up to 3 times a day. Kalorie Premium gives you 10 scans per day with no ads. Food search and barcode scanning are unlimited.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Open the app and go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data and can't be undone. If you're unable to access the app, email us at support@kalorie.fit instead.
